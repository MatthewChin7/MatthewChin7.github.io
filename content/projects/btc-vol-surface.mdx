## Abstract

Institutional FX desks summarize an entire volatility smile with three
numbers: at-the-money volatility, a 25-delta risk reversal, and a 25-delta
butterfly. Crypto options markets quote the same structures, but with
thinner liquidity and 24/7 microstructure noise. This project asks whether
those three observables, plus a sensible interpolation model, are enough to
reconstruct a usable BTC implied-volatility surface — and where the
reconstruction breaks.

## Context

A full options surface is a function $\sigma(K, T)$ over strike and expiry.
Quoting desks compress each expiry's smile into three structures:

$$
\mathrm{RR}_{25} = \sigma_{25C} - \sigma_{25P}, \qquad
\mathrm{BF}_{25} = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\sigma_{25C} + \sigma_{25P}\right) - \sigma_{\mathrm{ATM}}
$$

The risk reversal measures the smile's skew; the butterfly measures its
convexity.<MarginNote index="1">In BTC these have a physical
interpretation too: persistent demand for downside puts shows up as a
negative risk reversal in stress regimes.</MarginNote> The question is what
gets lost in this compression — and whether the standard reconstruction
machinery (Vanna–Volga adjustments, SVI and SSVI parameterizations) that
grew up around FX transfers to a market with jumps, funding quirks, and
inverse-contract conventions.

## Method

1. **Recover the three anchor points** per expiry from quoted structures,
   converting between delta and strike space under the market's convention.
2. **Interpolate the smile** three ways and compare: Vanna–Volga
   (replication-based, three-instrument exact fit), raw SVI per expiry, and
   surface-level SSVI with calendar no-arbitrage constraints.
3. **Evaluate** each proxy against the full Deribit strike ladder that the
   compression discarded, checking both pricing error and no-arbitrage
   violations (butterfly and calendar).

## What exists today

- Data collection for Deribit quotes and structure extraction.
- SVI per-expiry calibration with penalty terms for butterfly arbitrage.
- Vanna–Volga pricer for the 25-delta triangle.

Cross-model evaluation and the write-up are in progress.

## What I would do next

Extend the comparison across regimes (calm vs. stressed weeks), and test
whether the butterfly quote adds measurable information over a two-point
(ATM + RR) reconstruction — a model-selection question as much as a
markets one.
